## How you sign it

Add a `Signed-off-by:` line to your commits (`git commit -s`). That is the Developer
Certificate of Origin, plus this file. There is no form to fill in and no PDF to print.

## Why a project this small bothers

Two reasons, both dull and both real.

If every contributor holds an unlicensed copyright in part of the file, the project can
never change licence, never offer commercial terms to fund the work, and never respond to a
licence being found unenforceable somewhere — because it would have to find and re-contact
every contributor who ever fixed a typo.

And a standard that anyone may implement will eventually be implemented by someone with
lawyers. Clean provenance is what lets this project survive that conversation rather than
quietly disappear during it.
